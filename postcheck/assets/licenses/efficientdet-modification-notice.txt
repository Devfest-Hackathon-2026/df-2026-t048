efficientdet_lite0.tflite is the unmodified EfficientDet-Lite0 detection
model published by the TensorFlow authors.

whiteboard_experiment.tflite and docs_experiment.tflite are modified versions
of EfficientDet-Lite0: the PostCheck team fine-tuned (changed the weights of)
the pretrained model on Open Images Dataset V7 photos with TFLite Model Maker
0.4.3 in November 2026, and added new labels (whiteboard; picture_frame,
poster). See the "Open Images Dataset V7" entry for the training data.
